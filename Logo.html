02 — Construction

The mark One stroke, one bead. A J that holds.

Two pieces

A single round-capped stroke shaped like a J — the shank, the bend, the foot — plus a coral bead at the head. That's it. The bead is the only color in the mark; everything else carries the ink.

Proportions

The bead diameter is one unit (1x). The bowl of the hook is three units across (3x). Stroke weight is roughly the bead radius. These ratios hold at every size — we don't redraw, we just scale.

Reading

Reads as a hook, a J, a quiet question. Up close it's a fishing hook; at a glance it's the initial of a word that doesn't need finishing.

Clear space

Hold a margin equal to the bead diameter on all sides. The mark needs room to breathe — anything tighter and the bead starts to feel like punctuation belonging to whatever's next to it.
